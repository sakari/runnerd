Runnerd Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 August 2026

Runnerd is a running tracker app that measures the distance, duration, and pace of your runs using your device's GPS, and stores that history locally on your device. This policy explains what data Runnerd processes, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

Runnerd is developed by the Runnerd project ("we", "us"). If you have any questions about this policy, open an issue at github.com/sakari/runnerd/issues.

2. What data Runnerd processes

Runnerd accesses the following data on your device:

  • Precise location (GPS coordinates, speed, and timestamps), used only while a run is active, in order to measure distance and pace.
  • Run history you create in the app — start time, duration, and total distance for each run you record.
  • Optional tip purchases. Runnerd offers an optional one-off tip that supports development. It unlocks no features. The app contacts RevenueCat, our purchase-management provider, when it starts, when you open the History screen, and when you open the tip screen or make a tip. RevenueCat receives a randomly generated anonymous identifier for your installation, basic app and device information (app version, operating-system version, device model, country), your IP address, and the details of the purchase itself.

Runnerd does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or contacts.
  • Photos, microphone input, health data, or biometric data.
  • Advertising identifiers, device identifiers used for tracking, or cross-app/site tracking data.
  • Analytics, telemetry, or crash-reporting data.
  • Payment card details. Tips are charged by Apple or Google through your existing store account; Runnerd never sees or stores your payment information.

Your location and run history are never sent to RevenueCat or anywhere else. The tip feature and your run data are entirely separate.

3. How your data is used

  • To calculate the distance, pace, and duration of the run you are currently recording.
  • To display your run history and period summaries (week / month / year) inside the app.
  • To play audio callouts and scheduled local notifications at the halfway point and end of a timed run.
  • To show the correct localised price for the optional tip, to complete a tip you choose to make, and to remember that you have tipped so the app can thank you and stop asking.

Runnerd does not use your data for advertising, profiling, or training machine-learning models.

4. Where your data is stored

All run data is stored locally on your device in a SQLite database managed by the app. Runnerd does not operate any servers, and your location and run history are never transmitted to us or to any third party.

The one exception is the optional tip described in section 2. Records of a tip — the anonymous installation identifier and the purchase itself — are held by RevenueCat on servers in the United States, and by Apple or Google as the seller of record. A copy is cached on your device so the app knows you have tipped without needing a network connection.

5. Background location

When you start a run, Runnerd requests permission to use your location, including in the background. Background location is used only while a run is active so that distance and pace continue to be measured when your screen is locked or you switch to another app. iOS displays the blue status-bar indicator while background tracking is in progress. Background tracking stops as soon as you end the run.

6. Notifications

If you grant notification permission, Runnerd schedules local notifications on your device at the halfway and finish points of a timed run. These notifications are generated on-device. Runnerd does not use push notifications and does not send any data to a notification server.

7. Third parties and SDKs

Runnerd does not use third-party analytics, advertising, attribution, or tracking SDKs. Aside from the purchase provider below, the app uses standard operating-system APIs and open-source libraries (React Native, Expo, SQLite) that run locally on your device.

Runnerd includes the RevenueCat SDK solely to offer and manage the optional tip. It is contacted only for that purpose — to check whether you have already tipped, to fetch the current price, and to complete a purchase — but because the check runs when the app starts and when you open the History screen, it happens whether or not you ever open the tip screen. It receives the data listed in section 2, never your location or run history, and it is not used for advertising, profiling, or tracking you across apps or websites. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Revoke permissions at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services and Settings › Notifications. Runnerd cannot record runs without location permission.
  • Delete individual runs from the History screen in the app.
  • Delete all data by uninstalling Runnerd from your device. Because your runs and location data are stored only locally, uninstalling removes them completely.
  • Tip records are the exception. If you have tipped, the purchase record held by RevenueCat and by Apple or Google is not removed when you uninstall the app — that is what allows “Restore purchases” to work if you reinstall. To have the RevenueCat record deleted, open an issue at github.com/sakari/runnerd/issues.

If you are located in the EEA, the United Kingdom, or another region with comparable privacy laws, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of your personal data, and the right to data portability. Because Runnerd stores your data only on your own device and never receives a copy, you can exercise these rights directly through the app or by uninstalling it. If you believe we have nonetheless processed your data unlawfully, you may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

9. Children

Runnerd is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data to us, open an issue at github.com/sakari/runnerd/issues and we will take appropriate action.

10. Security

Because Runnerd stores your data only in the app's private storage on your device, its security depends on your device's protections (device passcode, biometric unlock, and Apple's app sandboxing). Keeping your operating system up to date and using a device passcode is the most effective way to protect your Runnerd data.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be available at this URL, and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the date of the most recent change. Material changes that expand how Runnerd uses your data will be announced in the app's release notes.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy or Runnerd's handling of your data: github.com/sakari/runnerd/issues.
